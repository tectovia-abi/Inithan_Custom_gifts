Data Deletion Instructions

Meta Platform Terms & GDPR Compliance • Inithan Custom Gifts

1. Overview

At Inithan Custom Gifts, we respect your right to control your personal data. In compliance with the Meta Platform Terms, WhatsApp Cloud API guidelines, and international privacy standards (GDPR / Indian DPDP Act), this page provides clear instructions on how you can request the permanent removal of your data from our systems.

2. How to Request Data Deletion

If you used your Facebook account, WhatsApp phone number, or Email to interact with Inithan Custom Gifts, follow these simple steps to request deletion of your information:

1

Submit an Email Request

Send an email from your registered email address to inithancustomgifts@gmail.com with the subject line Data Deletion Request - Inithan Gifts.

2

Include Your Account Identifiers

In the body of the email, include your full name and the phone number / email associated with your Inithan account or WhatsApp conversations so we can accurately locate your records.

3

Processing & Confirmation

Our Data Protection Officer will review and fulfill your request within 48 to 72 business hours. You will receive a formal confirmation receipt with a confirmation tracking code.

3. What Information is Deleted

Upon verification of your request, we permanently remove:

  • Your user account, hashed credentials, and contact details from our MongoDB database.
  • Your uploaded custom gift photos and mockups from our secure cloud storage (AWS S3).
  • Stored WhatsApp message histories, conversation logs, and notification records.

Note: Financial transaction invoices and records may be retained for the minimum period legally required by Indian tax authorities and accounting compliance.
