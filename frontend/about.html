Handcrafted With Care, Crafted For Memories

Inithan Custom Gifts was founded with a simple yet powerful vision: to make gift-giving deeply personal, thoughtful, and memorable. What started as a dedicated workshop has blossomed into a beloved custom gifts brand trusted by customers and organizations across India.

Whether it’s a thermal magic mug that reveals a cherished photo when hot coffee is poured, a laser-engraved 3D Spotify acrylic plaque playing your favourite song, or a curated employee welcome kit for your growing team — every single piece is handcrafted with precision, premium materials, and heartfelt love.

We take pride in turning your ideas and photos into stunning keepsakes that bring joy and smiles that last a lifetime.
