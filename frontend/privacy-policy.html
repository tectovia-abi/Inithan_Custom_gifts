Privacy Policy

Last Updated: November 10, 2026 • Effective Date: November 10, 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Inithan Custom Gifts (“we,” “us,” or “our”), accessible from inithancustomgifts.com. We are deeply committed to protecting your personal information and respecting your privacy.

This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, store, utilize, and protect your information when you visit our website, place orders for customized gifts, interact with our customer care, or receive communications via Email and the Meta WhatsApp Business Platform.

2. Information We Collect

When you use our store and services, we may collect the following categories of information:

  • Account & Contact Details: Full name, email address, mobile/WhatsApp telephone number, and delivery address.
  • Personalized Gift Media: Photos, custom names, songs, dates, and personalized messages uploaded by you to create personalized products (frames, mugs, plaques, etc.).
  • Transaction & Order Data: Items ordered, customization specifications, transaction amount, delivery status, and order identifiers. (Note: We do not store credit/debit card numbers or CVVs; all payments are processed through RBI-licensed payment gateways like Razorpay).
  • Communication Logs: Customer support messages, WhatsApp notification receipts, and verification request records.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information exclusively for legitimate e-commerce operations, including:

  • Fabricating, printing, and fulfilling your custom gifts according to your exact specifications.
  • Shipping and delivering physical products to your doorstep across India.
  • Sending essential transactional updates such as order confirmations, tracking links, and account security verification codes (OTPs) via Email and WhatsApp.
  • Providing dedicated customer assistance for customization proofs and revisions.
Our Guarantee: Inithan Custom Gifts never sells, rents, or trades your personal information or uploaded personal photographs to any third-party advertisers or data brokers.

4. WhatsApp & Automated Communications

We leverage the official Meta WhatsApp Cloud API to provide you with seamless customer updates. When you provide your phone number, you may receive:

  • Account security One-Time Passwords (OTP) for account login and password recovery.
  • Order status, live tracking links, and shipping notifications.
  • Preview proofs for custom engraved or printed gifts.

You can opt out of non-transactional WhatsApp notifications at any time by replying “STOP” or by contacting our team at inithancustomgifts@gmail.com.

5. Data Security & Storage

We implement strict administrative, technical, and physical safeguards to protect your personal data:

  • All web communications are encrypted with modern Transport Layer Security (HTTPS/TLS).
  • User passwords are one-way hashed using industry-standard bcrypt before storage.
  • Customization photos and digital proofs are securely stored in private cloud buckets (Amazon Web Services S3) with restricted access controls.

6. Your Rights & User Data Deletion

Under applicable data privacy regulations, you have the right to access, rectify, or request the permanent deletion of your personal data and uploaded photos from our active systems.

To request data deletion, please visit our dedicated User Data Deletion Instructions page or email us at inithancustomgifts@gmail.com with the subject line “Data Deletion Request”. All verified requests are completed within 48 to 72 hours.
